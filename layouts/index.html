About

A product manager who likes to look under the hood.

I have spent more than five years owning LOTUSim, an open-source multi-agent simulation platform used by academic and industrial teams across three continents. My role sits at the intersection of users, engineers, researchers and decision-makers.

My PhD and engineering background help me navigate technical depth. Product management is how I turn that depth into priorities, adoption and a story people can align around. Outside work, I build tools and visualisations whenever I find information that deserves a clearer shape.

From ambiguity to an operating view.
